# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project management approach with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and data-informed decisions. The project lifecycle moves from initiation and stakeholder alignment through planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. Teams rely on documented roles, backlog-driven delivery, acceptance criteria, risk tracking, stakeholder communication, and quality checks to deliver reliable increments and improve performance over time.

## Project Management Process Summary

### 1. Initiation
The team validates the business need, aligns stakeholders, defines success metrics, and decides whether the initiative should move forward into planning. The project charter / one-pager captures the problem, goal, timeline, risks, and resource needs.

### 2. Planning
Once approved, the team creates a prioritized backlog, estimates scope, defines milestones, and documents dependencies, resources, and the Definition of Done. This stage aligns stakeholders around scope, sequencing, and expected delivery.

### 3. Execution and Tracking
Team work is tracked through a delivery rhythm of standups, weekly reviews, backlog management, and project-board updates. Pull requests follow lightweight standards with clear acceptance criteria, automated checks, and review requirements before merge. Progress is monitored against milestones and measurable outcomes.

### 4. Risk and Communication Management
Risks, blockers, and cross-team dependencies are captured in a risk register and reviewed regularly. The team maintains stakeholder communication through status updates, issue escalation paths, and incident communication when needed.

### 5. Release and Deployment
Releases are planned with technical and operational readiness checks, including CI/security validation, smoke testing, rollback planning, and stakeholder notifications. This reduces deployment risk and improves observability.

### 6. Retrospective and Continuous Improvement
After each sprint, milestone, or incident, the team reflects on what worked well, what should improve, and what action items should be tracked. The goal is to turn feedback into manageable improvements with owners and follow-up.

### 7. Roles and Responsibilities
OctoAcme uses clear roles to support delivery: developers build and test the work, Product Managers define outcomes and priorities, Project Managers coordinate planning and communication, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. These roles support accountability, efficient handoffs, and alignment across the project.
